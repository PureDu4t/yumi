About Me 关于我

Yumi is a multimedia composer, arranger, sound designer. He is a jack of all trades, writing across a multitude of ensembles, including symphonic orchestras, orchestral hybrids, chamber groups, modern rhythm sections, world instruments and vocal groups; and styles such as world, pop, rock, jazz, and classical. Influenced by his experience living in China, Zimbabwe, Uzbekistan, and The United States, his works resemble a fusion of cultures and embrace an extensive use of ostinatos. 俞米是一位多媒体作曲家、编曲家和音效设计师。他是一位多面手，擅长为多种乐队配置创作，包括交响乐团、融合乐队、室内乐团、节奏乐器组、世界乐器和声乐团体；风格涵盖世界音乐、流行、摇滚、爵士、古典等。受其在中国、津巴布韦、乌兹别克斯坦和美国的生活经历影响，他的作品融合了多种文化，并广泛使用固定音型。你可以在他的音乐中听到拉丁节奏搭配日本术曲风格，唢呐与萨克斯重奏等奇妙的组合。

Over his two and a half years at Berklee, his arrangements and compositions have been performed at various renowned venues such as the South by Southwest Film Festival (SXSW), Boston Symphony Orchestra (BSO), Berklee Performance Center (BPC), Distler Performance Hall (Tufts), and Sosberg Recital Hall (Brandeis), summing up to a total of 51 times on stage. 在伯克利的两年半时间里，他的编曲和作品已在多个知名场所演出，包括西南偏南电影节（SXSW）、波士顿交响乐团（BSO）、伯克利表演中心（BPC）、迪斯特勒表演厅（塔夫茨大学）和索斯伯格独奏厅（布兰迪斯大学），以及伯克利其他多个演出厅，总计登台演出五十余次。
