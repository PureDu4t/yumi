Tower of Infinity

无限之塔

Genre:

类型：

Tactical Warchess/Visual Novel 战术战棋/视觉小说

My Role:

我的参与：

Music&SFX/Gameplay Design/Partial Translation 音乐&音效/游戏设计/部分翻译

You play as an architect trapped in an endless cycle, a divine punishment for humanity's ambition and hubris. Your goal is to fend off monsters sent by the gods to reach the top of the tower, only to build it further up to eventually reach heaven. 你扮演一位被困在无尽循环中的建筑师，这是对人类野心和傲慢的神圣惩罚。你的目标是击退众神派来的怪物，到达塔顶，然后继续建造塔楼，最终到达天堂。

Our gameplay is a combination of visual novel and tactical RPG. You engage in turn based chess-like combat while progressing through the story. Your choices in and out of combat both influence your ending and whether you're able to escape the loop. The story is inspired by Camus' "Sisyphean heroism," which believes that Sisyphus pushing the boulder is meaningful if he finds happiness doing it. Our music also uses loops until the final track, which only plays once, signaling breaking out of the loop(or not?) 我们的游戏玩法结合了视觉小说和战棋游戏。你在推进故事的同时参与回合制类似棋盘的战斗。你在战斗内外的选择都会影响结局以及你是否能够逃脱循环。故事灵感来自加缪的"西西弗斯式英雄主义"，认为如果西西弗斯在推石头时找到快乐，那么这个行为就有意义。我们的音乐也使用循环，直到最后一曲，只播放一次，象征着打破循环（或不？）

I Close My Eyes

我闭上了眼

Genre:

类型：

Audio Game/Maze 音频游戏/迷宫

My Role:

我的参与：

Conceptualization, Composer for levels 1, 2, 4, & main theme, Art 概念设计，第1、2、4关及主旋律作曲，美术

I've always dreamed of designing audio games where player location can be precisely told. Over the years, I have come up with a working navigation system using audio and "I Closed My Eyes" was my first attempt in implementing that. The game uses a sfx navigation system, where horizontal and vertical location is represented by one pitch followed by another delayed pitch. The pitches ascend and descent according to the pentatonic scale. This was done to avoid dissonance and make player movement sound like a melody on top of the underscore, which is groove oriented and has no melody. In addition to the location navigation system, we designed the "blind cane," which allows the player to hear the surrounding 8 tiles in clockwise motion. The quality of the tiles are represented with sound effects. For example, if there is a ghost on the tile, the player would hear "ooo." Currently, the game is only functional till the 2nd level, but you can still hear how our complete audio navigation system and "white cane" system works. 我一直梦想设计能够精确传达玩家位置的音频游戏。 多年来，我想出了一个可用的音频导航系统， "我闭上了眼"是我首次尝试实现这一想法。游戏使用音效导航系统， 其中水平和垂直位置由一个音高和另一个延迟音高表示。 音高根据五声音阶升降。这样做是为了避免不和谐音， 让玩家移动听起来像无旋律背景上的旋律。 除了位置导航系统，我们还设计了"盲杖"， 允许玩家按顺时针方向听到周围8个格子的声音。 格子的属性用音效表示。例如，如果格子上有幽灵，玩家会听到"呜"声。 目前游戏只到第2关，但你仍可以听到我们完整的音频导航系统和"白杖"系统的工作原理。

Jumpy Frog

跳跃蛙

Genre:

类型：

B Game/2D Platformer B类游戏/2D平台跳跃

My Role:

我的参与：

Everything 全部工作

This game was made for my "Mixing For GAIMS" class at Berklee. I was tasked to create a walkable map that showcases basic implementation of layering, branching, and sfx triggering in unity. I went beyond the requirements and designed a B-Game, featuring an awkward physics system where the frog bounces up and down automatically, and the player has to rotate the frog using left and right arrow keys to aim the frog so that it bounces in the desired direction. 这款游戏是为我在伯克利的"游戏音频混音"课程制作的。 我的任务是创建一个可行走的地图，展示Unity中分层、分支和音效触发的基本实现。 我超出要求设计了一款B级游戏，采用了一个诡异的物理系统， 青蛙会自动上下弹跳，玩家必须使用左右方向键旋转青蛙， 以便它朝着期望的方向弹跳。
